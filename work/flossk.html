Flossk — Iraqi Money Assistant

First Iraqi budget app with a voice assistant that understands your dialect — free, offline, no ads.

  • Voice assistant (80+)
  • Offline-first
  • Web · Android · iOS
Year
2025
Role
Mobile Apps — Flutter
Stack
Voice assistant (80+) · Offline-first · Web · Android · iOS

Problem

Iraqi users manage money with paper or foreign apps that ignore the dinar, local habits and the Arabic language.

Solution

Income, expenses and deductions with an 80+ command voice assistant, multi-wallet, debts, 11 currencies with IQD default and reports — all stored locally, working offline.

Result

A 100% Arabic, offline-first money app: data stays on the device, everything runs by voice, free forever.
