SPEC / Process

How a job runs

T+0

Briefwhat & why

You describe the problem in your own words. I repeat it back, add what you left out, and we agree the target before anything is priced.

T+2D

Plan & quotescope, cost, dates

A written scope: pages or screens, features, what is excluded, one fixed price and real dates. The number in this document is the number on the invoice.

T+1W

Buildweekly preview link

Work happens in the open — a preview link you can click every week, built from the Arabic layout first rather than translated from English afterwards.

T+3D

Deliverhandover, not a black box

Deployed, tested on real devices, handed over with the accounts, keys and a short written guide. You own the repository, the domain, the hosting.

CONT

Supportthe desk stays open

The same desk that answered during the build answers after it — server faults, printer queues, camera records and the next feature.

Every stage is one message away — ask at any point and you get the answer the sheet already shows.

07 / FAQ

Questions, answered

What exactly do you build?

Full stack websites and dashboards, Flutter apps for iOS and Android, network and server infrastructure, and the brand design around them — usually all four for the same client.

Do you work in Arabic and English?

Yes. Every product I ship is designed in both — Arabic with full right-to-left support, English for international audiences.

How do projects start?

Send a message or book a call. We talk about the problem, I send scope, price and timeline, and work starts once you approve. No surprise costs.

How long does a project take?

A business site: 1–2 weeks. A full platform or app: 4–10 weeks depending on modules. Infrastructure and support work is scheduled around your hours.

Do you maintain what you build?

Yes — updates, monitoring, backups and fixes after launch, as a monthly retainer or per request.

Where are you based?

Baghdad, Iraq (UTC+3). I work remotely with clients anywhere and handle on-site work across Baghdad.

Does this site use cookies or analytics?

No cookie is set here and no analytics vendor is in the path. The counter keeps one salted hash of an ip address per Baghdad day, so the same visit cannot count twice, and nothing that names anybody — the privacy page lists every piece of it.

Which languages can a product ship in?

Arabic and English as standard, both with the full layout in their own direction. This site itself is written in twelve languages from one translation file, so adding yours to a product is an edit, not a rebuild.

What do you need from me to begin?

What exists and what hurts: the pages, screens or systems involved, any logo and text you already have, and who approves the work. That is enough for a written scope, price and dates — and the work starts when you say so.

Do I see the work while it is still being built?

Yes: a preview link every week rather than a reveal at the end. Every stage is one message away, and the answers are on this sheet before you have to ask.
