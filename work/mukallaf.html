Mukallaf — Iraqi Tax System

Full Arabic tax platform for Iraqi companies, accountants and taxpayers — seven tax modules in one place.

  • 7 tax modules
  • Arabic RTL
  • PDF & Excel export
Year
2026
Role
Full Stack Web Development
Stack
7 tax modules · Arabic RTL · PDF & Excel export

Problem

Tax work in Iraq was done by hand: wrong brackets, missed deadlines and avoidable fines.

Solution

Seven modules (payroll, annual settlement, corporate, contracts, real estate, land, free professions) with brackets per Income Tax Law 113/1982, official form generation, deadlines, penalties, roles and Excel/PDF export.

Result

One Arabic platform where every return is calculated, generated and tracked — from data entry to a printable official form.
