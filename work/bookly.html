Full Stack Web Development
Stack
Wallet & refunds · Supplier dashboard · Arabic RTL

Problem

Booking farms and chalets in Iraq runs on phone calls and DMs — no clear prices, no refunds, no trust.

Solution

A four-step booking flow, a digital wallet with instant refunds, loyalty points, and a supplier dashboard for calendars, prices and payouts.

Result

Guests book and pay with a clear summary and get refunds to the wallet instantly; suppliers run everything from one dashboard.
